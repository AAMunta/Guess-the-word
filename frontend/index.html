RULES

How to Play

  • Guess the hidden English word using the given word length.
  • A letter is revealed when both the letter and its position are correct.
  • After an unsuccessful guess, the game shows wrong-position and not-present letters.
  • You can use a limited number of clues, depending on your level.
  • After using all your clues, you may choose to forfeit the game.
  • Your points depend on the result of the game.

Detailed Rules

1. Guessing the Word

Each game has a hidden English word. Use the displayed word length to make a guess. Your guess must have the correct length and belong to the game's word list.

If a guessed letter matches the secret word at the same position, that letter is revealed on the word board. Letters already revealed in their correct positions are not reported as wrong-position or not-present letters.

After an unsuccessful valid guess, the game displays wrong-position letters and letters that are not present in the remaining unrevealed word. Repeated letters in these result lists are shown only once.

Invalid guesses do not reveal letters, but they count toward your total attempts and the counter that makes clues available.

2. Levels and Clues

Each level provides a limited number of clues. A clue reveals one unrevealed letter in the word.

Level Name Maximum Clues
1Easy2
2Normal2
3Intermediate3
4Advanced3
5Expert4
6Master4
7Legend5

A clue becomes available after five unsuccessful attempts. Using a clue resets the unsuccessful-attempt counter. The clue button appears when a clue is available.

3. Forfeiting

Once all clues for the current level have been used, you can choose to forfeit. Forfeiting ends the game, reveals the word, and deducts points based on your level.

N.B.: If the word is completely revealed through wrong guesses, the match ends automatically and the player earns 50 points.

4. Points

Correctly guessing the word: You earn points based on your level, reduced by 15% of the base points for each clue used.

Revealing the whole word through wrong guesses: You earn a fixed 50 points.

Forfeiting: You lose 25 points multiplied by your level.

Winning points

Base points = Level × 100

Final points = Base points × (1 − 0.15 × clues used)

Forfeit penalty = Level × 25 points

5. Unlocking Levels

Your available level depends on your total points. Reach the following totals to unlock each next level.

  • 500 points — Level 2: Normal
  • 1,200 points — Level 3: Intermediate
  • 2,000 points — Level 4: Advanced
  • 3,000 points — Level 5: Expert
  • 4,200 points — Level 6: Master
  • 5,500 points — Level 7: Legend
